# v5.0 Beta 1 Testing Checklist

1. Activate the theme and Source More Platform plugin.
2. Confirm the homepage displays: hero, trust strip, challenges, solutions, calculator, why Source More, industries, process, AI advisor, resources, about, and final CTA.
3. Test English and Arabic modes.
4. Test desktop, tablet, and mobile widths.
5. Confirm navigation menus and mobile Escape-key closing.
6. Confirm Fleet Savings Calculator calculations and floating shortcut.
7. Confirm WhatsApp opens with the configured number and message.
8. Confirm the floating AI Assistant loads through `wp_footer()`.
9. Test the homepage “Open AI Advisor” button. The plugin launcher must use one of the supported assistant selectors; otherwise the floating launcher remains the primary entry point.
10. Check the browser console and WordPress debug log for errors.
