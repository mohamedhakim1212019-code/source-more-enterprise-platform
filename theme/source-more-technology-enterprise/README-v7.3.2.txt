Source More Technology Enterprise Theme v7.3.2
================================================

Purpose
-------
Bilingual homepage content management and Arabic marketing/RTL polish.

What changed
------------
- Added a Homepage Showcase Content meta box to the English and Arabic front-page translations.
- Each Polylang homepage stores independent marketing copy.
- Added editable hero, section headings, descriptions, CTAs, and optional hero/about image URLs.
- Added stronger Arabic marketing defaults written for the Egyptian and regional B2B market.
- Improved Arabic hero typography, line-height, wrapping, and mobile responsiveness.
- Moved AI Assistant, Fleet Calculator, and WhatsApp actions into one RTL stack on the left.
- Preserved the same brand lockup composition in English and Arabic.
- Retained the stable sticky-header/admin-bar fix from v7.3.1.

Editing the homepage
--------------------
1. Open Pages > Home (English) or Pages > الرئيسية (Arabic).
2. Scroll to the Homepage Showcase Content meta box.
3. Open any section, edit the fields, and click Update.
4. Leave a field blank to use the built-in default copy.
5. The English and Arabic pages are edited independently.

Notes
-----
- The visual card structure remains controlled by the theme for consistency.
- Main marketing copy, buttons, and key images are editable per language.
- Source More Platform v3.7.1 is not changed by this theme release.
