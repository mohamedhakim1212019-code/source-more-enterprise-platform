Source More Technology Enterprise Theme v7.3.1
================================================

Scope
-----
Focused header stability hotfix for the bilingual showcase theme.

Changes
-------
- Keeps the sticky header below the WordPress admin toolbar for logged-in users.
- Prevents the top of the logo from being hidden while scrolling.
- Keeps the logo lockup orientation identical in English and Arabic.
- Corrects the mobile navigation panel and overlay offsets.
- Corrects mega-menu and anchor scrolling offsets.

Test checklist
--------------
1. Log in to WordPress and open the front end.
2. Scroll from the top to the middle and bottom of a long page.
3. Confirm the complete logo remains visible at all times.
4. Repeat in English and Arabic.
5. Log out or use an Incognito window and confirm the header remains at the top.
6. Test desktop, tablet, and mobile navigation.
